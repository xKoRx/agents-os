# concepts — Clutifx Chapter 06 (Failure Swing)

## C1 — Failure swing (definición de este capítulo)

Cuando el precio **no puede hacer un nuevo alto** (o, en espejo, un nuevo bajo): falla al intentarlo y **va directo al lado contrario**, sin llegar a ese extremo y romperlo [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0004|c-0004]] [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0046|c-0046]]. Término dicho literalmente en el capítulo ("failure swing", [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/transcript#^s-000000|s-000000]]); garbles ASR del propio video: "fellow swing", "felo", "celu", "pelo", "Fearless", "Finishing" — todos [ASR] preservados. Contraste con lo enseñado antes (tomar el alto y reaccionar) [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0005|c-0005]]. Definición previa del curso: ch01 (claims c-0098–c-0103 y documentation §6 de ch01-introduccion; [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/GLOSSARY|GLOSSARY]] §Failure swing); ejemplos previos: [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch02-time/CLAIMS|CLAIMS de ch02]] (ejemplo en semanal) y [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch03-bias/CLAIMS|CLAIMS de ch03]] (mención en directo).

## C2 — La función: contexto, no predicción

El failure swing **añade contexto** ("para saber mejor qué está pasando") [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0003|c-0003]]; no se predice ni se anticipa [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0016|c-0016]] [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0030|c-0030]]; advertencia explícita contra la obsesión [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0002|c-0002]] [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0062|c-0062]].

## C3 — Los tres motivos

Taxonomía enseñada en el capítulo: (1) **SMT** — el par correlacionado sí tomó el extremo y este no, "la más obvia y la más fácil" [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0008|c-0008]]; (2) **final de hora/final de vela** — el extremo se hizo en la parte final de la vela/hora y el algoritmo lo interpreta como hecho [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0010|c-0010]] [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0014|c-0014]]; (3) **brokers** — feeds distintos: otro broker sí lo tomó [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0020|c-0020]] [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0021|c-0021]] [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0025|c-0025]]. Con negación: ver el patrón horario no obliga a failure swing [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0011|c-0011]].

## C4 — Final de hora = final de vela

El "final de vela" no es el minuto exacto de cierre: en 4 h puede ser 4.15/4.20; es "la parte final de esa vela" [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0014|c-0014]]. El esquema en pantalla rotula "FINAL DE VELA" con velas de las 0100 y 0500 ([05:59] [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/frames/f-000559.png|f-000559]] [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/frames/f-000659.png|f-000659]]) [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0022|c-0022]]. Aplica en 1 h (2:55), 5 m (5:50/2:55) y 8 h (16:20; :00 con vela a :01) [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0036|c-0036]] [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0044|c-0044]] [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0052|c-0052]] [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0056|c-0056]].

## C6 — Rango activado por failure swing

La vela fallida **se puede considerar un rango** y **activado** "ni que en algunos casos no haya tomado el High" — target válido [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0007|c-0007]] [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0028|c-0028]]; permite entrar sin esperar la activación por el extremo opuesto [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0019|c-0019]]. Habilita objetivos no marcables sin el concepto [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0057|c-0057]].

## C7 — Identificación: reacción + order block + motivo

Sobre la marcha: la vela sube (se da por hecho el nuevo extremo "porque es como funciona el algoritmo" [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0017|c-0017]]), falla, reacciona, y con un motivo visible se declara el failure swing [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0029|c-0029]] [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0018|c-0018]]; el **order block** creado por la reacción: lo que podría ser el cambio de tendencia [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0038|c-0038]] [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0041|c-0041]] [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0047|c-0047]].

## C8 — Failure swing y rangos a favor (operativa final)

El trade se arma sobre **rangos a favor** (reiniciado contrario que se reinicia, o rango a favor creado), prefiriendo **rangos pendientes** sobre reiniciados [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0058|c-0058]] [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0059|c-0059]] [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0060|c-0060]]; la "lógica de los failure swings": varios encadenados crean los rangos de 8 h y 1 h que estructuran el trade [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0061|c-0061]].

## C9 — Temporalidades para leerlo

El motivo se ve mejor en **1 hora** [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0040|c-0040]]; el **5 minutos** da la hora exacta del extremo [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0036|c-0036]] [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0044|c-0044]]; en **30/15** ya hay rangos para vender [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0013|c-0013]] [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0047|c-0047]]; los ejemplos gruesos en **8 horas** [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0052|c-0052]].

## C10 — SMT vs failure swing (frontera)

El instructor clasifica un caso ambiguo: "me pinta un poco más a SMT, porque no pinta que sea el final de la vela, pero vamos a mirarlo igualmente" [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0055|c-0055]] — y resuelve que también era final de vela (:00 con vela a :01) [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0056|c-0056]]. La SMT es motivo de failure swing [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0008|c-0008]] y por eso este capítulo es corto: "con la SMT ya lo habéis visto" [[10-projects/Personal/Multimodal Knowledge Engine/knowledge-library/clutifx/ch06-failure-swing/CLAIMS#c-0027|c-0027]].
